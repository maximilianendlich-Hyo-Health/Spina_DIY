Schlüsselblatt · your private key sheet

Which figures stand for which letter under your current key. Keep this to yourself. The tool cycles through the variants in order; when painting by hand you can also pick any of a letter's figures.

The three levels

Schrift
One fixed figure per letter, word by word. Anyone with the chart below reads it at once; without the chart, a patient puzzler cracks it by counting.
Rätsel
Your passphrase secretly reassigns 57 figures to the letters. Common letters get several figures (E has four), words run together on one spine, and the chart below no longer applies. Hard, but solvable by a determined group with enough text, like the Zodiac 408 cipher. One wrongly painted figure only spoils one letter.
Tresor
The passphrase shuffles the alphabet and shifts every letter by a different amount, with decoy figures mixed in. Without the passphrase, practically unreadable. One wrongly painted figure scrambles everything after it.

For a Rätsel that is solvable in a lifetime: use the same key for the whole series, aim for 300 to 500 figures across all works, and leave one foothold, for example a title that is the first words of the hidden text. Use a long passphrase or the New key button, so nobody can guess the key and skip the puzzle.

Plain alphabet (Schrift)

Reading direction: vertical spines run top to bottom, columns left to right. Horizontal spines run left to right, lines top to bottom; the figures are the same, turned a quarter to the left. A spine opens and closes with a free dot. On one spine, a word break is a short horizontal bar between the closing dot of one word and the opening dot of the next, all three on the centre line. In Rätsel mode some figures carry a small open bead at the foot of the spine; those are separate figures.
